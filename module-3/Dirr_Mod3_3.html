5 Things I've Learned Since the Course Started

  • Software development involves much more than coding; planning, documentation, testing, and communication are all critical parts of the process.
  • Different software development methodologies, such as Agile and Waterfall, each have strengths and are suited for different types of projects.
  • Clearly defined requirements are essential because they help ensure developers and stakeholders share the same expectations.
  • Teamwork and communication are vital skills in software development since projects often involve collaboration among multiple people with different roles.
  • Continuous learning is necessary in the technology field because tools, programming languages, and best practices are constantly evolving.
